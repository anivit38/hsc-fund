====================================================================
 HSC ENDOWMENT — HOW TO USE
 Investment Club mock fund · https://hsc-fund.web.app
====================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A paper-trading platform for the school investment club. Nobody's
real money moves — the fund started with $1,000,000 of pretend cash
and everything else (stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities, crypto,
private-market units) is tracked the same way a real fund would:
analysts research and pitch ideas, a Portfolio Manager reviews them,
the CIO executes at the weekly Investment Committee, orders fill at
the next session's opening price, and the fund's NAV (Net Asset
Value) is recalculated every trading night using real market prices
pulled live from Yahoo Finance.

Four roles, in order of how much they can do:

  ANALYST   — researches and writes pitches ("I think we should buy
              X because...").
  PM        — reviews pitches, approves or rejects them, sets the
              target position size. Can't approve their own pitch.
  CIO       — runs Investment Committee, executes approved pitches,
              can trade directly (Quick Trade), manages members and
              risk limits, sees everything.
  ADVISOR   — the faculty sponsor. Read-only, but can comment.

Anyone can pitch any asset class — a stock analyst can just as
easily pitch a piece of real estate or a bond. There's no lane
restriction; the only real limits are the fund's actual risk rules
(position size, sector concentration, cash reserve, etc.), which the
system enforces automatically and shows you before every trade.


GETTING AN ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://hsc-fund.web.app
2. Click "Create account" (top of the sign-in card).
3. Enter your name, any email address, and a password (8+ characters).
4. You'll land on a "pending approval" screen. This is normal — every
   new account needs a thumbs-up from the CIO before it can see or
   do anything. Ask whoever is CIO to approve you from the Members
   screen (usually takes a minute).
5. Once approved, refresh or sign back in — you're in as an Analyst.
   The CIO can promote you to PM, CIO or Advisor later from Members
   if that's your role in the club.

Forgot your password? Ask the CIO to reset it for you from Members —
there's no self-service "forgot password" flow for a group this size.


THE BASICS EVERYONE SEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
DASHBOARD   Fund's total value (NAV), how it's done since inception,
            a chart of the fund vs. the S&P 500, and a breakdown of
            what the fund owns by asset class and sleeve (category).

HOLDINGS    Every position the fund currently holds — quantity,
            average cost, current price, gain/loss, weight of the
            fund. Sortable and filterable by asset class.

ALL PITCHES An archive of every idea anyone has ever pitched, with
            search and status filters (submitted, approved, rejected,
            executed, shelved, expired).

LEADERBOARD Who has contributed the most P&L, submitted the most
            pitches, and gotten the most approved — bragging rights.

LETTERS     Monthly performance letters to members, auto-drafted
            from real fund data and edited/published by the rotating
            analyst or the CIO.


IF YOU'RE AN ANALYST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW PITCH
  Pick any ticker from the whole universe (stocks, ETFs, bonds, real
  estate, commodities, crypto, private funds), write your thesis,
  and — this part is required — write a FALSIFIER: a specific,
  measurable thing that would prove you wrong. No falsifier, no
  submission. It's the single most important field in the form; a
  pitch without one is a hunch, not a thesis.

  Also fill in a price target, a stop price, and a suggested position
  size (as % of the fund). You can save as a draft and keep editing,
  or submit straight away once everything is filled in.

MY PITCHES
  Everything you've written — drafts, submitted, approved, rejected,
  executed. Click into any of them to see PM comments and the
  decision.

LESSONS LEARNED (post-mortems)
  When a position you originated gets fully sold, the system creates
  a pending post-mortem for you automatically. You have to explain
  what happened and — separately — whether your THESIS was right or
  wrong (independent of whether you made money; "right thesis, lost
  money" and "wrong thesis, made money by luck" are both real
  outcomes worth recording honestly). You can't start a new pitch
  until an overdue post-mortem is filed — mildly annoying on purpose.


IF YOU'RE A PM
--------------------------------------------------------------------
REVIEW QUEUE
  Every pitch waiting on a decision, fund-wide (not just "your"
  sleeve — any PM can review any pitch, just never their own). Open
  one, ask questions in the comment thread if something's unclear,
  then Approve (with a target weight and a note) or Reject (with a
  note explaining why).

SECTOR VIEW
  Pick any sleeve from the dropdown to see its positions, return vs.
  its benchmark, and the fund-wide analyst leaderboard.


IF YOU'RE THE CIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------
TO APPROVE (IC Agenda)
  Every PM-approved pitch waiting for you to execute at Investment
  Committee. Open one, hit "Preview risk checks" to see exactly what
  the trade would do to the fund's exposure, then Execute (or pass —
  the pitch goes to "shelved"). If a risk limit would be breached,
  you can still execute with a required override note; it's logged.

CONTROL CENTER (formerly "Pending Orders")
  This is also where QUICK TRADE lives — the direct way to buy or
  sell anything without going through the pitch process at all. Pick
  a ticker, buy or sell, size it by target weight or exact quantity,
  preview the risk checklist, execute. Useful for rebalancing or
  acting fast; it goes through the exact same risk engine as an
  executed pitch. Below that is the full order book — every order
  ever placed, with a Cancel button on anything still pending.

RISK PANEL
  Live view of every position's weight, sector exposure, and
  asset-class exposure against the fund's actual limits. Also where
  you force-exit a position (requires a note, logged as an override)
  or mark a security halted/delisted.

MEMBERS
  Approve pending signups (there's a highlighted section at the top
  for these), change anyone's role or sleeve, deactivate an account,
  reset a forgotten password, or add someone directly yourself.

HISTORY (Audit Log)
  Every trade, override, approval, and role change, timestamped and
  attributed. Also visible to the Advisor.


HOW A TRADE ACTUALLY HAPPENS (either path)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. An order is created (from an executed pitch, or Quick Trade).
2. It sits as "pending_open" until the next trading session.
3. Overnight, the system fills it at that session's REAL opening
   price (not today's price — this stops anyone gaming a delayed
   quote, and matches how a real fund would actually trade).
4. The fund's cash and holdings update; NAV is recalculated that
   night using real closing prices.

Every listed security (stocks, ETFs, bond funds, commodity funds,
crypto ETFs) gets its price from the real market via Yahoo Finance —
genuinely live, not simulated, unless the market data is briefly
unreachable, in which case that one ticker quietly falls back to a
simulator so the fund keeps running. Direct real estate and
private-market holdings are valued once a month, same as a real fund
would carry them, because there's no live public price for a piece
of property.


ACCOUNT SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Change your password anytime from "Account settings" at the bottom
of the sidebar. Do this the first time you log in if you were set up
with a default/shared password.

There's also a light/dark mode toggle in the top-right of every page
(sun/moon icon) — cycles Dark → Light → Auto (follows your device).


QUESTIONS OR SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Talk to the CIO first (they can fix roles, approvals, and passwords
directly from Members). Everything the fund does is logged in
History, so if something looks off, that's the first place to check
what actually happened and who did it.
